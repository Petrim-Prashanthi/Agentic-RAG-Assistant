# Acme Cloud FAQ

## Refund policy
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are prorated. Refunds take 5-7 business days.

## Pricing
The Starter plan costs $12 per month. The Pro plan costs $49 per month and includes priority support. Annual billing gives a 20% discount.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST. Pro customers get 24/7 chat support.
